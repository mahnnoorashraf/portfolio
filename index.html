About Me

I build intelligent systems that learn from data and solve real problems, from the dataset all the way to deployment.

I'm an AI/ML engineer with industry and research experience across machine learning, deep learning and computer vision. I enjoy the whole lifecycle: cleaning and curating data, training and evaluating models, and shipping them behind clean APIs and interfaces.

Alongside classical ML and neural networks, I work with large language models, building retrieval pipelines and intelligent assistants on top of them. I care about results that hold up outside the notebook.

Based in Islamabad, Pakistan, and open to on-site, hybrid and remote roles.
